# Punters HQ — Staged roadmap

This roadmap separates low-risk improvements from features that may require licensing, paid services or legal review.

## Stage 1 — Protect and verify the MVP
- [x] Export the complete AppDeploy source snapshot into this repository.
- [ ] Prove a repeatable clean restore/build/deploy process from GitHub.
- [ ] Run hands-on Android and desktop checks for the main workflows.
- [ ] Fix defects and record test outcomes.
- [ ] Verify the app icon and PWA install experience.
- [ ] Make race notes persist safely, with clear privacy wording.
- [ ] Improve tracker backup/restore and explain that records are device-local.

## Stage 2 — Improve punter tools
- Better performance summaries: strike rate, net result, return on stake and breakdowns by bet type.
- Filters and date ranges for the personal tracker.
- Manual race cards with meeting, race, runners and notes.
- Saved speed-map notes and reusable race-day checklists.
- More lessons on form, barriers, distance, class, jockey/trainer stats and track conditions.
- Clear source/date labels for imported information.

## Stage 3 — Research external racing information
Compare Australian coverage, update frequency, data fields, licensing, commercial redistribution rights, cost, reliability and API terms before selecting a provider.
Potential categories: fields/form guides, official scratchings and track conditions, results/dividends, jockey/trainer statistics, speed maps/sectionals, odds comparison and promotions where permitted.
Do not scrape or redistribute protected data without permission. Do not claim data is live until a licensed provider is integrated and verified.

## Stage 4 — Optional accounts and cross-device sync
Only after privacy and security design. Provide user-controlled accounts, secure sync, data export and account deletion. Do not require signup for the basic public experience unless the product decision changes.

## Stage 5 — Optional paid features
- Decide the free-versus-paid boundary first.
- Prototype in Stripe test mode only after explicit approval.
- No live charges or subscriptions until pricing, cancellation, refunds, tax and support are agreed.
- Keep core safer-play information available without payment.

## Stage 6 — Advanced research
Licensed live race data and odds; streaming only where rights permit; statistical models with transparent limitations and independent evaluation; moderated community; AR/VR only if user benefit and cost justify it.

## Product principles
- Mobile-first and fast on Android.
- Plain-language screens.
- No guaranteed-win claims.
- Distinguish user records, sample data and licensed external data.
- Safer-play controls must not encourage increased betting.
- No paid infrastructure without a clear reason and user approval.
